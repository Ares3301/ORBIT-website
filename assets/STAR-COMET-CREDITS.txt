Changes: retain HIP line segments only, add Japanese names from NAOJ, connect coordinates from HYG. Lines are not official IAU boundaries.
HYG Alula Australis (Xi UMa) record without a HIP field is matched to line endpoint HIP 55203.
Constellation Japanese names: https://www.nao.ac.jp/new-info/constellation.html

Comet elements: JPL Solar System Dynamics ELEMENTS.COMET, retrieved 2026-09-06.
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/dat/ELEMENTS.COMET
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sb/elem_tables.html
1P/Halley: element epoch MJD 39875, JPL 75.
2P/Encke: element epoch MJD 59897, JPL K273/12.
67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko: element epoch MJD 57305, JPL K284/1.
Positions calculated using fixed two-body Keplerian orbits, not precision ephemerides.

Dwarf planets: NASA/JPL SBDB API, full-precision elements retrieved 2026-09-07.
https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/sbdb.html
Objects: 134340 Pluto, 1 Ceres, 136199 Eris, 136108 Haumea, 136472 Makemake.
Per-object element epochs are retained in dwarfs.js. Fixed two-body approximation; colours/shapes are schematic, not photographic maps.
Audio: original oscillator-based ambient score and interface sound design. No third-party recordings or songs.
